College of Arts, Media and Technology (CAMT), Chiang Mai University The College of Arts, Media and Technology (CAMT) is an autonomous college under Chiang Mai University (CMU), a leading public institution in northern Thailand. It was founded to strategically develop human resources in the region, focusing on equipping students with advanced technological and information system expertise. A key emphasis is enhancing international competitiveness, particularly to drive growth in Thailand’s northern tourism, handicraft, and software industries. Guided by the philosophy of "producing graduates, postgraduates, and doctorates who can utilize knowledge at an international level," CAMT is committed to fostering a knowledge-based economy and society. It is recognized as a College of Excellence in Creative Digital Innovation, with a vibrant community of over 1,700 students and 160 faculty and staff members.
